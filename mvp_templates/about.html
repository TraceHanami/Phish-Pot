How It Works

1. Feature Analysis

The system analyzes 10 critical website characteristics:

  • URL Structure: IP addresses, length, and suspicious patterns
  • SSL Security: Certificate validity and encryption status
  • Domain Information: Age, registration details, and history
  • Content Analysis: Links, anchors, and page structure
  • Reputation Data: Google indexing and security reports

2. Machine Learning Classification

A Random Forest classifier trained on over 11,000 website samples makes the final determination:

  • High accuracy rate of 96.7%
  • Balanced precision and recall
  • Robust against false positives

3. Risk Assessment

The system provides:

  • Clear safety verdict (Legitimate or Phishing)
  • Confidence scores and risk percentages
  • Detailed explanation of risk factors
  • Safety recommendations when needed

Important Disclaimers

⚠️ Use as a Guide Only

This tool provides assessments based on machine learning analysis. While highly accurate, it should not be your only source of security information. Always exercise caution online.

🚫 Not a Security Guarantee

No automated system is 100% accurate. New phishing techniques emerge constantly, and legitimate sites may occasionally be flagged. Use your judgment and verify through official channels.

✅ Best Practices

  • Always verify website URLs before entering sensitive information
  • Look for secure connection indicators (HTTPS, padlock icon)
  • Be suspicious of urgent requests for personal information
  • When in doubt, contact the organization directly through official channels
